Our Services

Shared Living

Shared Living offers adults with developmental disabilities the opportunity to live in a supportive, family-like home with a dedicated provider.

This arrangement includes help with daily tasks, encouragement to build life skills, and the chance to participate in the community—all while being part of a home where they feel safe, valued, and supported.

Residential Respite

Sometimes, families need temporary support they can trust. Residential Respite provides short-term, out-of-home care for individuals with developmental disabilities in a safe and nurturing environment.

Whether it’s for a few hours, an overnight stay, or a longer visit, your loved one will receive personalized care and attention from trained professionals.

This service offers families the flexibility to handle personal responsibilities, take a trip, or simply rest while knowing their loved one is in capable and compassionate hands.

Non-Medical Transportation

We help individuals get where they need to go—safely and reliably.

From work and doctor’s appointments to community events, we offer flexible non-medical transportation options. Whether your loved one needs hands-on assistance during the trip or prefers to travel independently, we’re here to make every ride simple and stress-free.

Adult Day Support

Our Adult Day Support program offers engaging and enriching activities designed to help individuals build skills, connect with peers, and explore their interests in a safe and supportive environment.

Families can have peace of mind knowing their loved one is spending their day in a place that values care, growth, and meaningful connections.

Personal Care

Every family is unique, and so are their needs. Our Personal Care services are designed to provide the right level of support for your loved one at home. Whether you’re looking for a helping hand or prefer to direct care yourself, we’re here to make it easier.

  • Homemaker Personal Care: Our team provides assistance with daily tasks like meal preparation, light housekeeping, and personal hygiene, helping your loved one live comfortably and independently at home.

  • Participant-Directed Personal Care: Puts families in charge, allowing you to choose and manage caregivers while we handle the details like training and payroll.
